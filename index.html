About Us

A Comfortable 3BHK Homestay in Fort Kochi, Made for Families

Grace 3BHK HomeStay is a clean, spacious and fully-furnished 3BHK home in Fort Kochi, Kerala. Whether you are travelling with family, friends or colleagues, you get the privacy of an entire home rather than a single hotel room.

Enjoy a fully equipped kitchen, free WiFi, parking and friendly local hosts – right in the heritage heart of Fort Kochi, close to the Chinese fishing nets, Fort Kochi beach and the cafés and galleries of Fort Kochi.
